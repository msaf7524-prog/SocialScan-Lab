SocialScan Lab - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction

SocialScan Lab respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, and handled when using our service.

2. Information We Access

SocialScan Lab may process publicly available social media information and information that users explicitly authorize through supported platform integrations, including TikTok.

3. TikTok Data

When a user connects a TikTok account, SocialScan Lab only accesses information permitted by the user and the permissions granted through TikTok's authorization system.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide account analysis, display authorized profile information, generate security indicators, and provide recommendations related to account security.

5. Data Security

SocialScan Lab takes reasonable measures to protect authentication credentials and user information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. Data Sharing

SocialScan Lab does not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide the service, comply with applicable law, or meet platform requirements.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Authorized account information is retained only as necessary to provide the service. Users may disconnect their account or request deletion of information associated with their use of SocialScan Lab.

8. Third-Party Platforms

Use of third-party platforms such as TikTok is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of those platforms.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SocialScan Lab develops or its features and legal requirements change.

10. Contact

Questions or requests regarding privacy or deletion of information may be submitted through the official SocialScan Lab project channels.
